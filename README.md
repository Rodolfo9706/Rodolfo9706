### 👨‍💻 About Me

Hello! I'm **Rodolfo Isaac Verdín Monzón**, a Mechatronics Engineer and **Ph.D. Candidate in Mechatronics & Mechanical Design** at the **Center for Research in Optics (CIO)** in León, Guanajuato. My research centers on bringing machines to life by combining **soft robotics**, **fully actuated aerial manipulators**, and **nonlinear control for power electronics**.

- 🎓 **Ph.D. Candidate** focusing on Mechatronics and Mechanical Design at **CIO**.
- 🚁 Specializing in **Soft Aerial Robotics (QuadSoft, Soft-PVTOL)**, **Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAVs)**, and **Visual Servoing**.
- ⚡ Expert in **Nonlinear Control for Power Converters** (Bilinear Buck-Boost, AC/DC Rectifiers) using Lyapunov stability and adaptive control.
- 🛠️ Hands-on hardware builder: From mechanical CAD/FEA design to real-world experimental validation (HIL/SIL).

### 🚀 Areas of Research & Expertise

| Research Area | Focus & Applications |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🪱 **Soft Robotics & Bio-inspiration** | Soft aerial platforms (QuadSoft, Soft-PVTOL), proprioceptive continuum robots with sensorized tendons |
| 🚁 **Aerial Robotics & UAVs** | Fully actuated quadrotors, teleoperated aerial manipulators, PX4 integration, VR/teleoperation mapping |

### 📂 Selected Publications & Key Research Projects

1. 🚁 **The QuadSoft & The Soft-PVTOL** — *Design, construction, and experimental validation of soft and actuated quadrotors with passive elastic arms.*
2. 🪱 **Worm-inspired Proprioceptive Continuum Robot** — *Continuum robot with retractile motion and sensorized tendons for bio-inspired locomotion (Published in Mechatronics, Elsevier).*
3. ⚡ **Adaptive Control for Bilinear Converters** — *Lyapunov-based adaptive control with semi-saturation mechanism for bilinear buck-boost converters (Published in IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems I).*
4. 👁️ **Barrier Lyapunov Function Control for UAVs** — *Position-based visual servoing of fully actuated UAVs integrated into PX4 (Presented at ICUAS).*
